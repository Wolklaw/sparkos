# SparkOS branding

**The SparkOS bot:** a small robot with a mint visor and a spark on its antenna, hammering a glowing bar on an anvil and throwing sparks. It's original artwork for SparkOS, in the wolklaw house colours: indigo, mint, and the magenta/cyan slip as the only decoration.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `sparkos-bot.svg` | The static mark, caught at the moment of a blow, with sparks and the magenta/cyan slip. For the logo and app icon. |
| `sparkos-bot-animated.svg` | Plays once at sign-in. The hammer lands its blows on the startup sound's beats (three blows with a rebound, then the heavy final one), sparks fly on each, and the image slips out of register on the last blow. Respects reduced motion. |
| `preview.html` | Not in git. Built by `python3 tools/make-bot-preview.py`: the animation and sound in one page; press *Play with sound*. |

Status: draft artwork. Phase 3 (PLAN.md) puts it in the boot splash, sign-in screen and app launcher, after the mockup is agreed.
